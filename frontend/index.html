How it works

From resume to feedback in four steps

  1. 1

    Pick your interview

    UPSC board, software, core, CA, MBA, bank, consulting, or paste any job description.

  2. 2

    Brief the panel

    Add your resume and GitHub. The panel reads your code and shows you what it knows.

  3. 3

    Answer out loud

    Each member has their own voice. They follow up on vague answers and cut you off when you ramble.

  4. 4

    Get feedback

    A score, what was missing in every answer, your pace and filler words, and what to prepare next.
